# 🦺 MT Generator

**AI-Powered Poster Creator for the Mining Sector**

MT Generator is an intelligent content generation platform that automatically creates **monthly safety, health, environmental, wellness, and managerial posters** tailored for the **mining industry**. It uses AI to combine **industry regulations, seasonal trends, and user inputs** to produce visually appealing, compliant, and relevant designs — saving time and ensuring consistent communication across mining operations.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Auto-Generated Posters:** Generate ready-to-print posters for safety, environmental, and wellness topics every month.  
- **Mining Act Integration:** References South African mining standards, acts, and safety regulations.  
- **Custom Themes:** Posters adapt to **current events, seasons, and user-selected topics**.  
- **Visual Templates:** Uses AI to produce eye-catching, professional poster layouts.  
- **Editable Exports:** Download or edit generated posters before final approval.  
- **Multi-Language Support:** (Planned) Generate posters in English, Afrikaans, or isiZulu.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Select your **poster type** (Safety, Environmental, Health, etc.).  
2. Add your **mine name or department** (optional).  
3. MT Generator analyzes current trends, laws, and your inputs.  
4. The system outputs a **beautiful, compliant, AI-generated poster** — ready for printing or digital display.
